Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Winnow has no servers and collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reporting.

  • Your photos stay on your device. Winnow reads your photo library (or the folders you pick) to show them to you, and moves the ones you delete to the system trash.
  • Uploads go only to the services you set up yourself (Immich, Google Photos, PhotoPrism, Synology Photos, Nextcloud/WebDAV, SFTP, S3, B2, Dropbox, OneDrive, Apple Photos albums), straight from your device to them. Their own privacy policies apply there.
  • Sign-ins and keys for those services are stored on your device only.
  • Supporter purchase (Play Store) is handled by Google Play; Winnow only learns whether you bought it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jamesfpate/winnow/issues.
