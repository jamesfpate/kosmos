How it works

Swipe, don't scroll

One photo at a time, oldest or newest first. Bursts come up together so you keep the sharp one. Undo anything.

Albums as you go

Winnow suggests the album by date: the trip you made last week, or the year's catch-all. Search, or make a new one in a tap.

Only keepers get backed up

Deletes go to the phone's trash. Kept photos upload into the same albums on your server, and space is freed once they're safe.

Integrations

Turn on as many as you like. Kept photos sync into albums of the same name on each; rename an album or move a photo in Winnow and it follows everywhere. A file that's already there is recognised, not uploaded twice.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, everything. If it saves you time you can sponsor it, or buy the Supporter thank-you in the Play Store version. Neither unlocks anything.

Where do my photos go?

Only to the services you set up, straight from your device. Winnow has no servers and collects nothing. Privacy policy.

Does it delete photos for good?

No. Deleted photos go to the phone's Recently Deleted / Trash, and you review the trash in Winnow before anything is removed. "Free up space" only removes kept photos once every backup has them.

Why not just use the cleaner apps in the store?

They delete, then leave the rest in one pile. Winnow files what you keep into albums and gets them onto your server, without a weekly subscription.

Is it open source?

The source is on GitHub under the Functional Source License: free to use, change and share, not to resell. Each version becomes Apache 2.0 after two years.
