Features

Albums by dateDated albums like “2026-07 Hawaii” are suggested for photos taken in their range, so a trip files itself.
A catch-all for the restPhotos that aren’t part of anything go to the year’s album (“2026”), or the month’s if you prefer.
Album searchFind any album as you type, or make a new one from what you typed. Drag a photo onto an album to file it there.
Albums stay in stepRename an album or move a photo in Winnow and the phone and every service follow.
Browse by albumSee everything on the phone, what you’ve reviewed, or any one album, and refile photos in bulk.
Bring your albumsImport the albums you already have in Immich, and renames made there come back to Winnow.

Integrations

On the device
iPhone and Mac Apple PhotosKept photos go into real albums in your Photos library, which iCloud syncs to your other devices. Deletes go to Recently Deleted.
AndroidDeleted photos are removed from the phone (via its trash, restorable for 30 days). Turn on Phone albums and kept photos move into a folder per album, which Samsung Gallery and other gallery apps show as albums.
Off the device
ImmichAlbums plus tags (Albums/2026/07), shared with family as editors. Browse your Immich library by album and refile from Winnow.
Google Photos AndroidLet Winnow manage what syncs to Google Photos: only photos you’ve reviewed and kept are uploaded, once, into albums of the same name. Turn off backup in the Google Photos app so nothing else goes up.
PhotoPrism · Synology PhotosReal albums on your own server.

Features

Only the keepersDeleted photos never upload. Kept ones go up with their album, and move if you refile them.
No duplicatesA file that’s already on the server is recognised and adopted, not uploaded twice, even after a reinstall.
Uploads your wayIn the background on Android, on Wi-Fi only if you like, with or without videos, per service.
Free up spaceOnce every backup has a kept photo, Winnow can remove it from the phone, or keep the last 30 days.
Nothing lost by accidentDeletes wait in Winnow’s trash for you to review, then go to the phone’s Recently Deleted.
Private by designNo account, no ads, no analytics: uploads go straight from your device to your services, and Winnow has no servers. Settings and albums export to a file you keep. Privacy policy

S3-compatibleAWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Wasabi, MinIO.
Backblaze B2Straight into a B2 bucket.
DropboxInto a folder in your Dropbox.
OneDrivePersonal or work OneDrive.

Layout on file storage: Photos/2026/2026-07 Hawaii/IMG_1234.jpg, with the year’s catch-all in Photos/2026/. Apple Photos albums live in your own library, so they don’t count as a backup.
