winnow trace

Image lines light up (keep from the bottom up, delete from the top down), then deleted photos fade out in place; kept photos stay. Tune everything in Settings below; the demos use your values right away.

Fade

The photo, its lines and its check all fade together. Deleted photos go grey as they fade.

Grid mode

Checked photos are kept, the rest deleted, all at once. Tap a photo to check or uncheck it.

Settings

These carry over to the app as-is: tell me the values you like, or copy them with the button below.

Stay: a kept photo stays where it is; its lines trace over it and fade. Only deleted photos fade out.
Along the lines: each outline in the photo draws itself like a pen. Sweep: the lines are revealed by a band moving up (keep) or down (delete).
